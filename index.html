Our Story

Four friends. One sound.

We started in a basement in 2019 with nothing but three chords and too much ambition. Since then we've played 200+ shows across the Northeast, released two EPs, and somehow still all like each other.

Our music pulls from everything — rock, soul, noise, silence. We don't fit neatly into one box, and we're fine with that.
